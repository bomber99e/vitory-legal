Consumer Health Data Notice

Effective date: 4 November 2026

For people in Washington State and Nevada, under Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law. It comes from Shlomo Shalev Peretz (SSP), Hatomer 20, Beit Shean, Israel, who runs VITORY.

Consumer health data we collect, and why

  • Body measurements and fitness: age, sex, height, weight, weigh-ins, activity level, training habits, goal and pace, and the calorie, protein, carb and fat targets worked out from them. We use these to calculate your targets, build your plan and suggest changes.
  • Allergies, intolerances and diet: allergies you select or type, gluten-free or dairy-free choices, your diet, religious food rules and dislikes. We use these to filter recipes and build your plan. Some of these may indicate a health condition.
  • What you tell our support chat, if it concerns your health. We use it to answer you.
  • That you confirmed our 18+ and health check. We use it to run that check. We don't record any specific condition.

Where it comes from

From you, in the app. We don't get health data from Apple Health, Google Fit, data brokers or other companies, and we don't infer health conditions from other data.

Who we share it with

We don't sell consumer health data, and we don't share it with third parties or affiliates. We have no affiliates. It goes only to the processors that run VITORY for us, which are companies providing database and login hosting, web hosting, and AI support chat (if you choose to use it). They may use it only for that work. They are named in our Privacy Policy. We would also disclose it if a valid legal process required us to.

Your consent

We ask for your consent, on its own in the app, before we collect this data. Support chat asks separately before anything goes to its AI provider.

Your rights, and how to use them

  • Confirm and access. You can see your data in Profile and download it from Profile → Export my data, or ask us whether we hold your data and for a list of the third parties and processors that have received it.
  • Withdraw consent. Use Profile → Health data consent, or email us.
  • Delete. Use Profile → Delete account, or email us. We delete your consumer health data and tell our processors to delete theirs, including from archives and backups within the time the law allows.

Email support@vitory.app from your account's address. We reply within 45 days, or tell you if we need more time, as the law allows. There is no charge for up to two requests a year.

Appeals. If we refuse a request, email us with the subject "Appeal" and we will reply in writing within 45 days. If your appeal is refused, you can complain to the Washington State Attorney General or the Nevada Attorney General.
