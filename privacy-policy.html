3. Your consent to use sensitive details

Before the app asks for your body, allergy or food details, it asks you to tick a separate box agreeing to VITORY using them to work out your targets and build your plan. We keep a record of that consent. You don't have to agree, but we can't make a personal plan without it.

You can withdraw at any time in Profile → Health data consent. VITORY can't plan without these details, so for an account this deletes the account, and for a guest it clears your answers from the device. Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before. If you would rather keep your login and have only these details removed, email us.

4. Our legal bases (UK and EU)

  • Explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR) for health data and anything revealing religious belief, and for third-party AI support chat.
  • Performance of our contract with you for your account, the app's features, subscriptions and support.
  • Legitimate interests for security, fixing faults, preventing repeat free weeks, usage analytics that you can turn off, and defending legal claims.
  • Legal obligation for tax and accounting records and answering lawful requests.

VITORY works out your targets and plans automatically, using formulas you can see. This doesn't produce legal or similarly significant decisions about you, and you can change the inputs or the result at any time.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, per-user access rules in our database, hashed free-week records and restricted access to our systems. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise absolute security. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Everyone can: see and edit your answers in Profile; download your account data from Profile → Export my data; turn off usage analytics; withdraw consent; and delete your account. For anything else, including a copy of what the export leaves out, email support@vitory.app. We will reply within 30 days, or within the shorter period your law requires. We may need to confirm the request comes from your account email. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

UK and EU/EEA

You can complain to your data protection authority, such as the UK Information Commissioner's Office or the Irish Data Protection Commission.

Israel

Under the Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, you can inspect your information and ask us to correct or delete it. You can complain to the Privacy Protection Authority.

United States

Depending on your state, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete and get a copy of your data, and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising and certain profiling. We don't sell your data, use it for targeted advertising or profile you in ways that produce legal effects, and we use sensitive data only to provide the service you asked for. We honour these requests whether or not your state's law applies to a business of our size. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by emailing us, and we will reply in writing. You can use an authorized agent where the law allows. Washington and Nevada residents have the further rights in the Consumer Health Data Notice.

Canada, Australia and New Zealand

You can ask to access and correct your information, and you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial commissioner, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, or the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

10. Children

VITORY is for adults aged 18 and older and isn't directed to children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, including children under 13 as covered by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. If you think a minor has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Storage on your device

The app stores your answers, plan, meal log and settings on your device so that it can work offline. It also stores a random ID for the free week (in the Keychain, which survives deleting the app) and a random ID for guest analytics. These aren't used for advertising, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, so we don't respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version with a new date, and tell you in the app or by email if the change is material. If we want to use sensitive details for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent first.
